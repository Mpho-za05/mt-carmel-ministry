Our Story

Established on 18 October 2018.

Based on a vision from the founder, the ministry was established to spread the truth and knowledge of God, to fulfil God's purpose, who wants all people to be saved from the coming wrath, and to save souls from the pit of Hell (Hosea 4:6).

The main focus of the ministry is to teach God's people to be kind (Ephesians 4:32), to give (2 Corinthians 9:7) and to love (1 John 4:19).

One altar. One God. One church family in Benoni.

The Altar Rebuilt

Twelve stones, one people

Elijah took twelve stones — one for every tribe of Israel — and built a single altar back up from the ruins. It's the picture we carry into every gathering: many people, many stories, one altar, one Lord.

Our Values

What we stand on, together.

Three commitments shape our purpose, mission and everything we do as a church family.

1

Honour

We HONOUR the uniqueness and profound supernatural and natural gifts that shape this ministry, keeping us on the correct path to achieving our purpose, mission and values.

2

Pursue

We PURSUE righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, gentleness, peace and holiness with all people.

3

Driven & Guided

We are DRIVEN AND GUIDED by the Word of God through the HOLY SPIRIT in all our meetings, responses and actions — big or small, now and in the times to come.

Our Vision

A platform for the Body of Christ.

To create the platform for the Body of Jesus Christ which will enlarge the Kingdom of God through all available resources — bringing God to all the people who need His encounter, help, deliverance, healing, restoration and hope.

Enlarging the Kingdom

Encounter. Deliverance. Healing. Restoration. Hope.

Our Mission

Calling a wavering generation back to the altar.

To bring uncompromised, undiluted Gospel about the Kingdom of Heaven through the Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, to the community at large; to restore people to Jesus Christ from the Kingdom of Darkness and Hell, by exposing knowledge and the truth from the living Word of God about the Kingdom of Light. Effectively impacting the community with high living morals of love, empathy, dignity and deep compassion for Jesus Christ.
